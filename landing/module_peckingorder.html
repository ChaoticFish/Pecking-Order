Meet Pecking Order.

Who reports to whom, without the PDF.

Pecking Order turns a spreadsheet into a living org chart. Search for anyone, follow the chain of command, fold away the branches you don't need, and print or export exactly what you see. It keeps private details private, and it never lets a card's text get too faint to read.

Why it exists

Org charts are PDFs.

Somewhere there's a slide with tiny boxes, three versions out of date, that nobody can search. Updating it means finding whoever has the original file. Most org chart plugins swap that for a pile of scripts, a hosted service, or a layout that only works for companies shaped like a perfect pyramid.

Pecking Order reads one CSV or JSON file, the kind any spreadsheet or HR system can make, and draws it the way real organizations look: offices with deputies, chiefs of staff off to the side, assistants on the line, dotted-line managers, open roles. Update the file and the chart updates. Everything runs on your own site.

The companion

Pecking Order Studio.

Open Studio

The chart file has to come from somewhere, so Pecking Order comes with Studio: an editor that's one HTML file running in your browser, online or off. Click a card to edit it, drag someone onto their new boss, and save. In Edge and Chrome it saves straight back to the file you opened.

  • Straight from HR: open a CSV from your HR system and Studio matches its columns for you, managers by ID or by name. Salary and anything else you don't match stays behind.
  • Many at once: Ctrl-click a dozen cards and change their department, location or colors together.
  • What changed: compare this month's chart with last month's: added, moved, changed and gone, outlined on the chart.
  • From a PDF: a ready-made prompt for an AI tool turns an old PDF org chart into a Pecking Order file.

What's in the box

Every org chart has a pecking order.

Real shapes

Drawn like your org

Offices with deputies inside, beside or below, and teams with their own heading and email inside a card. Staff to either side, assistants on the line, dotted-line managers, open roles. Top to bottom or left to right.

Privacy

Details by access level

Choose who sees contact details, offices, photos and the rest. Hidden fields are removed before the page is sent, and one click locks the file's folder.

Finding people

Search, filter, focus

Search with jump-to, filters by department, location and role, a color key, branch focus with a trail back, and links straight to a person.

Export

Paper, if you must

SVG, PNG or a sharp, searchable PDF, made in the browser. Printing the page fits the whole chart on one sheet, with the file's date on it.

Readable

Contrast, handled

Give a card any background color and the text color picks itself to pass WCAG AA. Keyboard navigation throughout, and a plain list without JavaScript.

Up to date

New faces, open roles

New and Starting soon tags from a start date, Help wanted on open roles with a link to the posting, and the date the file last changed.

Photos

Plays well with others

Uses ShrinkRay's smaller copies of photos when it's installed, and opens them in Chaos Loupe. Initials otherwise.

Your words

Translate anything

Every label on the chart, down to the Sad mode jokes, can be translated or reworded in Joomla's language overrides. Add your own CSS classes to any card.

Two moods

Sparkle, or Sad.

Sparkle (the default)

Cards swim in from the middle as a school. Search hits ping like sonar. Bubbles rise up the chain of command when you click someone. Folding a branch reels it in, and opening it casts it back out. Deeper teams sit in deeper water, and a rooster keeps an eye on the top of the pecking order. Boss not a fan of the rooster? Each effect has its own switch.

Sad mode

One button and it all goes beige. No motion, no rooster, and a gentle reminder of where you are: "Welcome to the beige zone. Population: you." Visitors who've asked their device for less motion get the calm without the teasing. The choice is remembered.

For the technical crowd

Under the hood.

Works with
Joomla 5 and Joomla 6, PHP 8.2 or later, any template. It uses the Bootstrap classes Joomla templates already load. New versions arrive through Joomla's own Update screen.
Data
One CSV or JSON file from the Media Manager or any folder on your site (Browse opens the full Media Manager), flat or nested. Read once and cached until the file changes. Up to 5,000 people.
Weight
One deferred script, about 72 KB (27 KB gzipped), and one stylesheet, about 6 KB gzipped. No outside libraries, fonts or services.
Privacy
Fields are shown by Joomla access level and stripped on the server. Columns the chart doesn't use are never sent. No cookies or tracking; Sparkle or Sad is remembered in the visitor's own browser.
Accessibility
WCAG AA text contrast on any card color, real buttons and links, visible focus, arrow-key navigation, reduced motion honored, and a nested list without JavaScript.
Exports
SVG, PNG and vector PDF built in the browser, Letter or A4, with a title and the file's date. Only what that visitor can see.
Columns
id, parent, name, title to start. Then deputies, placement, roles, contact details, photo, colors, start date, job link, CSS class, and more, all optional.
Studio
One HTML file, no install, works offline. Opens and saves CSV or JSON, and is included in the module package.
